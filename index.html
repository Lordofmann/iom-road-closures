Mountain Road one-way operation

The A18 Snaefell Mountain Road operates as a one-way system in the racing direction (Ramsey Hairpin → the Bungalow → Creg-ny-Baa) from 4:30pm Friday 22 May until 9:30am Tuesday 9 June 2026. You cannot drive up the Mountain from Douglas / Creg-ny-Baa during this entire period, even when the course is technically open.

Extra full-closure windows for setup & teardown: The A18 is also fully closed (no traffic at all) from 9:30am to 4:30pm on Friday 22 May for cone and signage setup, and from 9:30am to 4:30pm on Tuesday 9 June for sign removal. These are short maintenance windows on top of the one-way and racing closures.
